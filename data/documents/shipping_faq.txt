TraceGuard Shipping and Delivery FAQ
Document ID: DOC-SHIPPING-002
Last Updated: February 2026

1. Standard Shipping: Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days across North America and Western Europe. Free on orders over $50.
2. Express Shipping: 1 to 2 business days expedited shipping is available for an additional $14.99 fee.
3. International Shipping: Available to over 60 countries. International transit time ranges from 7 to 14 business days depending on local customs clearance.
4. Tracking: Every shipment includes an end-to-end carrier tracking number emailed within 24 hours of dispatch.
5. Damaged Shipments: Report any physical carrier damage within 48 hours of delivery along with photographic evidence.
