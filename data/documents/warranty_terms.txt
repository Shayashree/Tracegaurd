TraceGuard Hardware Warranty Terms
Document ID: DOC-WARRANTY-003
Last Updated: January 2026

1. Warranty Coverage: All TraceGuard certified peripheral hardware items come with a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty.
2. What is Covered: Defects in materials and workmanship under standard personal or office use.
3. What is Not Covered: Accidental liquid spills, cosmetic wear and tear, unauthorized firmware modifications, or misuse.
4. Warranty Claims: Submit claims with serial number and proof of purchase to support@traceguard.local. Replacement units are dispatched within 3 business days of claim validation.
