TraceGuard Return and Refund Policy
Document ID: DOC-REFUND-001
Last Updated: January 2026

1. 30-Day Window: Customers may request a full refund or exchange within 30 days of product delivery.
2. Condition of Items: Items must be in original condition, undamaged, and returned with all original accessories and packaging.
3. Proof of Purchase: A valid order ID, invoice, or receipt is required for all returns.
4. Non-Refundable Items: Gift cards, downloadable software licenses, and clearance final-sale items cannot be returned.
5. Processing Time: Once received and inspected at our warehouse, refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days to the original payment method.
6. Return Shipping: Free return shipping labels are provided for defective items or fulfillment errors. For discretionary returns, the customer covers return shipping fees.
